Winnipeg Trucking FAQ

How do I request a freight quote?

Use the quote form on this page, email dispatch@calcuttalogistics.com, or call or message 431-277-8330. Dispatch reviews each request and replies with pricing for your freight.

Which areas does Calcutta Logistics serve?

We serve Winnipeg and the surrounding area, including our base in West St. Paul, as well as lanes across Canada and the United States. Popular lanes include Winnipeg to Toronto, Calgary, Vancouver, Chicago and Minneapolis.

Do you handle cross-border freight between Canada and the US?

Yes. Cross-border Canada-US freight is one of our core services. Tell dispatch the origin, destination and commodity so the shipment can be quoted and planned.

What equipment types do you offer?

We offer full truckload, less-than-truckload, reefer (temperature-controlled), flatbed, expedited, dedicated and contract hauling, and drayage. Contact dispatch to confirm the right equipment for your freight.

Can you move dangerous goods or hazmat freight?

Dangerous goods inquiries are welcome and are handled case by case. Send the product details, classification and lanes to dispatch and we will confirm whether we can assist.

Is dispatch available 24/7?

Yes. Dispatch is available 24/7 by phone and WhatsApp at 431-277-8330 and by email at dispatch@calcuttalogistics.com.

What is the fastest way to get a quote?

Call or message dispatch at 431-277-8330 with your pickup and delivery locations, freight type and weight. You can also submit the quote form, which sends the same details to dispatch.

What information should I provide for a quote?

Please include pickup and delivery city and province or state, freight type, approximate weight, the service you need, your preferred pickup date and your contact details. Any special handling needs help dispatch respond accurately.

What is the difference between LTL and FTL?

Full truckload (FTL) uses a trailer for one shipper's freight, while less-than-truckload (LTL) moves smaller shipments that do not require a full trailer. Dispatch can help you decide which suits your load.

How do I reach dispatch after hours?

Dispatch operates 24/7. Call or send a WhatsApp message to 431-277-8330, or email dispatch@calcuttalogistics.com, at any hour.
