HELPFUL ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions.

Common questions about our freight services, coverage and quote process.

How do I request a freight quote?+

Complete the quote form below, call 431-277-8330, or email dispatch@calcuttalogistics.com with your shipment details.

What areas does Calcutta Logistics serve?+

Calcutta Logistics serves Winnipeg and surrounding communities, destinations across Canada, and Canada–US freight lanes.

Do you arrange cross-border freight between Canada and the US?+

You can inquire about Canada–US cross-border transportation. Share the origin, destination, freight description, and available shipment documentation so dispatch can discuss coordination.

What freight transportation services are available?+

You can request information about FTL, LTL, cross-border trucking, reefer, flatbed, dry van, expedited, drayage, and specialized freight.

Can I inquire about dangerous goods transportation?+

Yes, contact dispatch with the proper shipping name, classification, and shipment details. Acceptance and any special requirements must be confirmed before booking.

Is dispatch available 24/7?+

Yes. Calcutta Logistics lists dispatch availability as 24/7. Call 431-277-8330 for shipment inquiries.

How can I get a freight quote quickly?+

Provide complete pickup and delivery locations, freight dimensions and weight, equipment needs, and your preferred pickup date.

What information should I provide for a quote?+

Include contact details, pickup and delivery cities and provinces or states, freight type, approximate weight, pickup date, and any handling instructions.

What is the difference between LTL and FTL?+

LTL generally shares trailer space with other shipments, while FTL generally reserves a trailer for one shipment. The right option depends on the freight and route.

How can I contact dispatch after business hours?+

Call 431-277-8330 or message the same number on WhatsApp. You can also email dispatch@calcuttalogistics.com.
